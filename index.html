How to Contact Us

To help us understand and resolve your issue, please include the following details in your email when relevant:

1. Describe the issue Tell us what happened and what you expected to happen.
2. Include your device details Provide your device model and iOS version.
3. Add steps to reproduce Explain what you tapped or did before the issue occurred.
4. Attach a screenshot if useful A screenshot can help us understand display or app errors.

Please do not include passwords, payment card details, or other sensitive personal information in your support email.

Support Availability

You can email us at any time. Support requests are reviewed and answered as soon as reasonably possible. Response times may vary depending on the nature and volume of requests.
